    Invoice Generator for Contractors: how it works

    Contractor invoices get disputed when labor and materials are lumped together or when a progress payment isn't reflected. QuickInvoice lets you list every item with a quantity and unit price, apply tax only where it belongs, and show the balance after the deposit, all on a PDF the homeowner or general contractor can forward to their bank.

    It is free, works in your browser on a phone or laptop, and keeps your business details, logo and saved invoices locally. No app to install, no account.

    Separating labor, materials and extras

    A clear contractor invoice groups work so the client can check it against the estimate:

    • Labor – one line per task or per trade ("Demo and haul-away", "Framing – 16 hrs × $65"). Hours as quantity, rate as unit price.
    • Materials – list major items with quantity and unit cost. If you add markup, either bake it into the unit price or show a "Materials handling 10%" line; don't hide it.
    • Permits, dump fees and equipment rental – pass-through costs on their own lines.
    • Change orders – anything outside the original scope, with the date the client approved it.

    Reference the estimate or job number and the property address in the notes. For sales tax, many jurisdictions tax materials but not labor; enter only the taxable portion or note the rule in writing.

    Progress billing and final invoices

    On jobs that run more than a couple of weeks, bill in stages: deposit, rough-in, and completion are common. Use a sequential invoice number for each stage (INV-0041, INV-0042) and mention the stage in the notes ("Progress payment 2 of 3 – drywall complete"). To show a deposit already received, apply it as a fixed discount so the balance due is explicit.

    On the final invoice, include a short statement of completion and any warranty terms ("Workmanship warranted for 12 months"). Set a due date that matches your contract; 7–14 days is typical for residential work, net 30 for commercial GCs.     Frequently asked questions

    Can I use this on a phone at the job site?

    Yes. The page is responsive and the PDF is created through your phone's share or print dialog. Choose Save as PDF and send it by text or email.

    How do I apply tax only to materials?

    The tax field applies a percentage to the subtotal, so if only materials are taxable, calculate that amount and enter it as a separate line, or note the tax basis in the notes field. For mixed jobs, many contractors bill materials on a separate invoice.

    Can I show the deposit the client paid?

    Set the discount to Fixed and enter the deposit amount. The invoice shows the job total, the deposit deduction and the remaining balance.

    Does the invoice include my license number?

    Put it in the address / tax ID field under your business details. It prints under your name on every invoice, which several states require for licensed trades.
